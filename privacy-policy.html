01Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install and use the ExpiryGuard — Important Items Expiry Reminder Android application. We value your privacy and are committed to being fully transparent about what data stays on your device and what limited technical information is processed by third-party advertising services integrated into the App.

By using ExpiryGuard, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02Information You Create & Store Locally on Your Device

ExpiryGuard does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or billing details. All core functionality operates locally on your Android device:

  • Tracked Expiry Items: Item names, categories (Food, Medicine, Documents, Warranties, Subscriptions, Other), expiry dates, recurring renewal intervals (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly), and optional text notes that you enter.
  • Reminder Configurations: Preset reminder timings (same day, 1, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days before expiry) and custom reminder configurations (custom number of days before expiry and specific notification time).
  • Item History & Archive: Records of items that have expired, been marked as completed/used, renewed, or moved to history.
  • App Preferences: Your local preferences (such as Light/Dark/System theme mode, global notification toggle, default reminder timing, automatic recurring rollover preference, and "Expiring Soon" day threshold) stored on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore.

All of the information listed above is stored exclusively in a local SQLite/Room database and local preferences inside ExpiryGuard's private application storage on your device. We do not have a backend server, and we cannot view, access, collect, or transmit your tracked items or notes.

03Item Photos, Camera & Photo Picker Usage

ExpiryGuard allows you to optionally attach a photo to an item (for example, a photo of a product label, receipt, medicine box, or warranty card).

  • Zero-Permission Photo Selection & Capture: When you choose Gallery Photo or Take Photo, ExpiryGuard uses the official Android system Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia) or delegates capture to your device's built-in system Camera app. ExpiryGuard does not request broad access to your entire photo library or external storage.
  • Strictly Local Storage: Any photo you attach is compressed and saved locally inside the App's private internal storage directory (item_photos) on your device.
  • No External Transmission: Item photos are never uploaded to any cloud server, never shared with third parties, and never used for analytics or advertising.
  • Automatic Cleanup: When you remove a photo from an item, permanently delete a record, use the Delete All Active & History Items option in Settings, or uninstall the App, the associated local photo files are permanently deleted from your device.

04Local Notifications, Reminders & Device Permissions

ExpiryGuard schedules expiry alerts directly on your device using Android's system alarm and notification services. Notification scheduling and delivery happen entirely on your device without using remote push notification servers.

ExpiryGuard requests only the minimum Android permissions required for its features:

Permission Purpose in ExpiryGuard
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Allows the App (on Android 13 and above) to display local reminder notifications when your tracked items are approaching their expiry date or expire. You can grant or revoke this permission at any time in Android Settings.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Allows the App to automatically restore and reschedule your active local expiry alarms after your phone or tablet restarts, ensuring you do not miss scheduled reminders.
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used strictly by the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to check network availability and load banner and interstitial advertisements. ExpiryGuard does not use your internet connection to transmit your personal item database.

05Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

ExpiryGuard is offered as a free application supported by advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The App displays banner ads and occasional frequency-capped interstitial ads.

Information Collected by Google AdMob

To display, measure, and improve advertisements and prevent fraud, the integrated Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data directly from your device, including:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Your device's Google Advertising ID (AAID) or similar mobile identifiers.
  • IP Address & Approximate Location: Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general/coarse geographic location (such as city or country).
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: Device model, operating system version, screen dimensions, language setting, mobile carrier, and app performance/crash diagnostics related to ad rendering.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Information about advertisements shown in the App (such as ad impressions and whether you tapped on an advertisement).

Important: Google AdMob does not have access to the item names, expiry dates, notes, categories, or photos you enter inside ExpiryGuard.

Managing Personalized Ads & Opt-Out Controls

You can control or reset your mobile advertising identifier and opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings:

  • Open your device's Settings app → Google → All services (or Privacy & security) → Ads.
  • Select Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID to limit ad tracking and personalization.

06Data Retention, Deletion & Your Controls

Because all item records, photos, reminders, and preferences are stored locally on your device, you have complete and immediate control over your data at all times:

  • Edit or Delete Individual Items: You can edit any item, remove its attached photo, cancel its scheduled reminder, or delete it at any time inside the App.
  • Clear History Log: You can permanently delete all archived/history records by going to Settings → Clear History Log or tapping the clear button on the History screen.
  • Delete All App Data: You can wipe all active items, history records, scheduled alarms, and saved item photos in one tap via Settings → Delete All Active & History Items.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling ExpiryGuard from your Android device immediately and permanently removes the local database, all saved item photos, preferences, and scheduled reminders from your device.

Please note that because we do not store backups of your data on external servers, once you delete your data or uninstall the App without an OS-level device backup, your items cannot be recovered by us.

07Data Security

Your tracked items, notes, and photos are stored inside Android's sandboxed application storage directory, which is protected by the Android operating system and inaccessible to other apps on non-rooted devices. Any network requests made by the Google Mobile Ads SDK are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) connections. We recommend protecting your mobile device with a strong screen lock (PIN, password, pattern, or biometric authentication).
